## **1. What is Jobster Conversion Tracking?**

Jobster's conversion tracking allows us to measure the number of completed applications on your career site originating from a Jobster campaign.

<aside>
➡️ Important: The Jobster conversion tag does not use cookies or store personal user data. It **ONLY** counts the number of conversions **generated after a click on a Jobster ad.**

</aside>

## **2. Why should you implement Jobster conversion tracking?**

By transmitting your conversion data to Jobster, the platform can make data-driven optimizations. These are based on the number of applications generated for each campaign, channel, and vacancy.

Once conversion tracking is enabled, you can see all the conversions generated by your campaigns directly in Jobster. You can combine this with all the available dimensions in the platform, including campaign names, publisher websites, vacancy titles, locations, job categories, and so on.

Jobster conversion tracking also provides the most reliable way to attribute and measure the conversions directly generated by your Jobster campaigns.

## **3. How to Implement Jobster Conversion Tracking?**

There are two methods for implementing Conversion Tracking:

### 1. Client Side

The advantage of the Client Side implementation is that it’s quick (+/- 5 minutes), and you can use it with limited technical knowledge by using Google Tag Manager. The downside is that Client Side conversion tracking can be less accurate because the Jobster scripts are sometimes blocked by Ad Blockers and some browsers.

### 2. Server Side

The advantage of using Server Side conversion tracking, or sometimes called “Postback tracking” is that it’s more accurate: it won’t be blocked by adblockers or browsers. The downside is that it requires somebody that is able to implement the Server Side tracker in the backend of your website. For an experienced developer though, it won’t take more than a few hours to implement.
