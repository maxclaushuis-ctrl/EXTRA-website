Reliability

We do not tolerate no-shows

A reliable staff member cancels properly. If they don't, there are consequences. This way you're never left with an unpleasant surprise.

Always call to cancel

Staff are required to cancel by phone. A text message or chat is not sufficient.

€50 fine for improper cancellation

Anyone who doesn't follow the protocol is fined €50. Immediate, consistent and fair.

2× improper = non-active

After two improper cancellations a staff member is suspended. No second chance for carelessness.

You're never caught off guard

Thanks to this protocol you know in good time when someone can't make it — so we can arrange a replacement.

Reliability is not an afterthought at EXTRA

Our cancellation protocol is not for show. It protects you as a client and ensures our staff take their responsibilities seriously.
