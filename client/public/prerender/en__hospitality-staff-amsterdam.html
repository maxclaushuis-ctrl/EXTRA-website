Cancellation protocol

No-shows are not tolerated

A reliable employee cancels properly. If they don't, there are consequences. That's how we make sure you never face surprises.

  • 01

    Always call to cancel

    Staff are required to cancel by phone. A text or message is not enough.

  • 02

    €50 fine for improper cancellation

    Anyone who doesn't cancel according to protocol gets a €50 fine. Immediate, consistent and fair.

  • 03

    Twice improper means suspended

    After two improper cancellations an employee is suspended. No second chance for sloppiness.

  • 04

    You never face a surprise

    Thanks to this protocol you know in good time when someone can't make it. So we can arrange a replacement.

  • 05

    Reliability is not a side issue

    Our cancellation protocol isn't there for show. It protects you as a client and makes sure staff take their responsibility seriously.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can you supply hospitality staff?

At EXTRA we know busy periods often come unannounced. Thanks to our large pool of experienced hospitality staff, we can usually deploy people fast. In many cases we can propose suitable staff within 48 hours for hotels, restaurants, events or catering jobs.

Which roles can I hire hospitality staff for?

Through EXTRA you can hire staff for a range of roles in hospitality. Think waiting staff, bar staff, runners, chefs, front-office staff and housekeeping. We always look at which people best fit the job and the venue.

What does hospitality staff through a staffing agency cost?

The cost of hospitality staff through a staffing agency depends on several factors, such as the role, experience and length of the assignment. At EXTRA we work with transparent rates and are happy to think along on a solution that fits your situation.

Why do businesses choose a hospitality staffing agency?

Many businesses choose a hospitality staffing agency because it offers flexibility. You can bring in staff quickly for busy periods, events or sickness. A staffing agency also takes recruitment, selection and admin off your hands.

Can you scale up flexibly for busy periods or events?

Yes. Flexibility is one of the main advantages of working with EXTRA. Whether it's a big event, a busy weekend or a temporary peak in demand: we can scale up and down fast with experienced hospitality staff.

What kinds of businesses does EXTRA supply staff to?

EXTRA supplies hospitality staff to a range of businesses in the hospitality sector, such as hotels, event venues, caterers and restaurants. That gives us plenty of experience with varied jobs and working environments.

How do I request staff from EXTRA?

You can easily request staff via the request form on our website. Once we've received your request, we'll get in touch to discuss your needs and planning.
