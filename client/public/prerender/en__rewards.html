FAQ

All about EXTRAATje

How quickly do I see my points?

After each processed shift, your points are added to the EXTRA app automatically. This takes no more than 24 hours.

Do my points expire?

No. Your points don't expire, they keep building up. You can redeem them whenever you like.

Can I transfer points to someone else?

No, points are personal and linked to your account. They can't be transferred.

How do the status levels work?

You start as a bronze employee. As you earn more points, you move up to silver (10,000 points), gold (15,000 points) and diamond (25,000 points). Higher levels unlock more exclusive rewards.

Can I join if I don't work for EXTRA?

The EXTRAATje system is exclusively for EXTRA employees. Not registered yet? Sign up below.
