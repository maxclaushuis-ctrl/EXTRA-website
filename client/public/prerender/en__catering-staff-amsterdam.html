FAQ

Frequently asked questions about catering staff

Can you supply staff for large catered events?

Yes. EXTRA has plenty of experience with large catering jobs. From small corporate dinners to events for hundreds of guests: we supply experienced waiting staff, runners, bar staff and kitchen support. Get in touch and we'll see straight away what's possible.

How quickly can you supply catering staff?

In many cases we can supply suitable catering staff within 48 hours. Thanks to our pool of experienced employees we can move fast when someone drops out or for last-minute catering requests.

Do you have staff with catering experience?

Yes. Many of our employees have plenty of catering experience and are used to changing locations. They know the dynamics of catering jobs: quick set-up, efficient service and a smooth breakdown.

Can you supply waiting and bar staff for catering?

Yes. We supply experienced waiting staff, dinner runners, runners and bar staff for catering jobs. Everyone is selected on catering experience, work attitude and presentation.

Do you also supply kitchen staff for caterers?

Yes. EXTRA also supplies kitchen support for caterers: kitchen assistants, dishwashers and kitchen porters who keep the kitchen running during large catering jobs.

Can we work with regular employees?

Yes. EXTRA works with favourites pools. For each catering business we build a fixed pool of employees who know your way of working, your standard and your pace. Less explaining, higher quality.
