NEN 4400-1 certification

EXTRA Uitzendbureau B.V. is registered under the NEN 4400-1 standard. On this page you can read what the standard covers and what it means in practice for clients and employees.

The standard

What is NEN 4400-1?

NEN 4400-1 is the Dutch standard for staffing agencies based in the Netherlands that supply workers to other businesses. It checks whether an agency meets its obligations on payroll taxes, VAT and the identity and right to work of its employees.

A NEN 4400-1 registration is audited independently, twice a year, and listed in the SNA register. That gives clients certainty: if you hire staff through a certified agency, you run far less risk of being held liable as the hirer for unpaid payroll taxes or VAT.

For clients

What this means for you as a client

  • Everyone who works through EXTRA is on EXTRA's payroll. No freelancer arrangements and no bogus self-employment.
  • EXTRA pays payroll taxes and VAT correctly, checked against the NEN 4400-1 standard.
  • Less risk of hirer's liability: you hire staff from an agency that has been independently audited.
  • Clear, verifiable agreements on rates, hours and invoicing.
