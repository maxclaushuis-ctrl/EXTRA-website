Hotel staff need more than skills. Presentation, attitude and hospitality shape the guest experience. So we select on all three.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about front office

How quickly can you supply front office staff?

In many cases within 48 hours. Thanks to our regular pool of selected hotel staff we can move fast when someone drops out, at peak times or for a last-minute request.

Which front office roles can I hire staff for?

Reception, check-in and check-out, guest relations, the night shift and concierge support. Tell us in your request which level and which languages you expect.

Can I ask for the same receptionists again?

Yes. We work with a favourites pool per hotel and per department, including front office. Staff you are happy with get priority in your schedule.

Can I reach you outside office hours?

Yes. EXTRA is available 24 hours a day, so you can act fast when someone drops out at the weekend or at night.

How do you monitor the quality of front office staff?

After every shift we measure how an employee performed at your hotel. Scores, compliments and complaints are logged, so we know who gets priority.
