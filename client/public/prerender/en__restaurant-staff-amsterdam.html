FAQ

Frequently asked questions about restaurant staff

Can you supply hospitality staff for a restaurant quickly?

Yes. In many cases we supply suitable hospitality staff within 48 hours. Thanks to our pool of experienced staff we can move fast when someone drops out, calls in sick or your restaurant is unexpectedly busy.

Do you have experienced waiting staff for restaurants?

Yes. Our waiting staff have extensive hospitality experience and are used to the pace of a busy restaurant. They know the etiquette, work independently and are well presented.

Can you also supply kitchen staff for a restaurant?

Yes. EXTRA supplies chef de partie level cooks, sous-chefs, kitchen porters and dishwashers. We select on kitchen experience and the ability to work independently in an unfamiliar kitchen from day one.

Can we work with regular staff through EXTRA?

Yes. Through the favourites pool system we build up a regular group of staff for each restaurant. They know your menu, your way of working and your quality standard. Less explaining, more quality on every shift.

Can you supply staff for weekends and peak periods?

Yes. Busy weekends, holidays and peak periods are exactly where EXTRA comes in. We supply flexible hospitality staff who are used to a high pace and fluctuating demand.

Are your staff on your payroll?

Yes. Everyone who works through EXTRA is on our payroll. We meet the NEN 4400-1 standard. No freelancer risk for your restaurant, no false self-employment.
