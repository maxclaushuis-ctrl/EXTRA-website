FAQ

Common questions about chef work

Do I need experience as a chef?

Yes, most chef roles require proven kitchen experience. We look at your level and match you with suitable kitchens. For supporting kitchen roles, less experience is sometimes enough.

Can I choose where I work?

You tell us your preferred kitchen style and location, and we look for kitchen jobs to match. You are never obliged to accept a fixed location.

How does payment work?

After your shift you log your hours in the EXTRA app. Once they're approved, your pay is ready the next day via Jixbee. No waiting until the end of the month.

Will I always work at the same location?

Not necessarily. Some chefs choose a regular pool at one hotel or restaurant, others prefer to pick up a variety of kitchen jobs. Both are possible with EXTRA.

How soon can I start?

After signing up and a short introduction meeting, you can pick up your first chef shifts within a week. We keep the hurdle as low as possible.
