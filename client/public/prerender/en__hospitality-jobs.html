FAQ

Everything you want to know

How exactly does daily pay work?

We process your hours after every shift. The next morning your wages are in your account. That applies at weekends and on public holidays too. No waiting, no monthly pay runs.

What happens at the introduction meeting?

The meeting takes about 30 to 45 minutes at our office. We talk about your experience, preferences, availability and personality. It's informal, but professional. We genuinely want to know who you are.

Do I need experience in hospitality?

Not necessarily. We work with people at every level. What we look for is the right attitude, a presentable manner and motivation. Experience helps, but your drive matters just as much.

How flexible can I be?

Completely flexible. You set your availability and choose which shifts you take. There's no minimum number of hours, although we recommend staying active for the best matches.

What are the chances to progress?

With EXTRA you can grow from one-off shifts to a regular assignment with one client. Employees with high scores and strong reviews are the first we approach for exclusive projects and regular roles.
