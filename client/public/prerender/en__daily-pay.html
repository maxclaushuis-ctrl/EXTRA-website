How does daily pay work at EXTRA?

After your shift, the client approves the hours you worked. Your wages are then processed automatically and paid into your account the next morning. You don't have to do a thing: no expense claims, no requests. In the app you follow your earnings per shift in real time, including evening and weekend supplements.

Good to know: daily pay is a choice, not an obligation. If you'd rather be paid weekly or every four weeks, that works just as well. And it all happens within a normal employment set-up: you build up holiday pay, you're insured and you get a proper payslip. Work today, paid tomorrow, no compromises.

Who is daily pay made for?

For anyone who wants to stay on top of their money. Students who plan their part-time job around lectures and don't want to wait a month for their first pay. Young professionals earning extra flexibly alongside their job. And anyone just starting with EXTRA: your first shift literally pays out the next day. Combine it with choosing your own shifts and you have a part-time job that fits completely around your life. Take a look at our part-time jobs in Amsterdam page too.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When will the money be in my account?

Once your hours are approved, your wages are processed automatically and are in your account the next morning.

Does daily pay cost me anything extra?

No. Daily pay is a service from EXTRA, not a paid option. You receive your full wages in line with the collective labour agreement (cao).

Do I still get holiday pay and a payslip?

Yes. Daily pay doesn't change your rights: you're on our payroll, you build up holiday pay, you're insured and you receive payslips as you should.

Can I choose weekly pay instead?

Of course. Daily pay is optional: you choose the pay frequency that suits you and can change it later.

Does daily pay apply to all shifts?

Yes, daily pay applies to all the work you do through EXTRA: hotels, restaurants, events and catering.
