FAQ

Common questions about front-office work

Do I need experience as a receptionist?

Some hospitality experience is a plus, but it isn't always required for front office work. We look at your communication skills, professional appearance and guest focus. For some front office jobs in Amsterdam we offer a short on-site introduction.

Will I always work at the same hotel?

In many cases, yes. We try to place you in a regular pool at one or more hotels, so you know the procedures and quickly feel at ease as a hotel receptionist in the team.

Can I choose my own shifts?

Yes. You let us know your availability and we arrange suitable front office shifts. That way you can combine front office work with your studies, another job or other commitments.

How does payment work?

After your shift you log your hours in the EXTRA app. Once they're approved, your pay is ready the next day via Jixbee. No waiting until the end of the month.

How soon can I start?

After signing up and a short introduction meeting, you can often start receptionist work within a week. We do our best to make your start as smooth as possible.
