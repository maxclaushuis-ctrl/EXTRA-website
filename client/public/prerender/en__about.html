What we stand for

Work should pay. Literally.

EXTRA is more than a staffing agency. We are a movement for a fairer, more enjoyable way of working in hospitality. Employees who put in the effort earn more, not just in pay, but in recognition and rewards too.

With our EXTRAATje points system we automatically reward employees for every shift, every challenge and every moment they go the extra mile.
