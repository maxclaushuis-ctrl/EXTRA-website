05References and permits

Three checks before the contract

A good interview isn't enough. Before anyone gets a contract, we check references, the right to work in the Netherlands and identity. If anything doesn't add up, it stops here.

Reference check

We call a previous employer and ask about reliability, attitude to work and the reason for leaving.

Reference good

Work permit

EU passport or a residence permit with the right to work. If a work permit is needed, we arrange it first and keep track of the end date.

Right to work valid

ID check in the contract portal

The identity document is verified in our contract portal before signing. Only once approved does the contract go out.

Identity confirmed
StopNot in order? Then the candidate gets no contract, however well the interview went.
