FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which sectors does EXTRA supply staff to?

EXTRA is a staffing agency in Amsterdam for hotels, events and logistics. We supply hotel staff to hotels, event staff to event venues, caterers and restaurants, and logistics staff for warehouses. Everyone who works with us is on our payroll.

Which roles can I hire staff for?

In hotels: housekeeping, front office, F&B, banqueting, chefs and dishwashers. At events: waiting staff, bar staff, runners, hosts and event supervisors. In logistics: order pickers, forklift and reach truck drivers, packers, dispatch and warehouse operatives. We only place forklift drivers with a checked, valid certificate.

How fast can you supply staff?

Busy periods often come out of nowhere. Thanks to our large pool of experienced staff, in many cases we can propose suitable people within 48 hours, for a hotel, an event or your warehouse. If someone drops out, we check straight away who is available.

Can you scale up at peak times?

Yes. Whether it's a big event, a fully booked hotel weekend or a seasonal peak in your warehouse: we scale up and back down quickly. Staff who do well with you go into your regular pool.

Do you also cover shift work and weekends?

Yes. Early, late and night shifts are possible, as are evenings and weekends in hospitality and at events. Allowances follow the hirer's remuneration rules under your collective labour agreement.

Do you work with freelancers?

No. Everyone who works through EXTRA is on our payroll and EXTRA is NEN 4400-1 certified. Payroll tax, collective agreement pay and insurance are our responsibility. So you run no risk of false self-employment.

What does staff through a staffing agency cost?

You pay an all-in hourly rate that depends on the role and the experience. That covers wages, contributions and insurance, with no hidden costs. Whether it's hospitality staff, event staff or logistics staff: request a quote for your situation.

How do I request staff from EXTRA?

You can easily request staff via the request form on our website. Once we've received your request, we'll get in touch to discuss your needs and schedule.
