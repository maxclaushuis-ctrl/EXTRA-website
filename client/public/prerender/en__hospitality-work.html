FAQ

Common questions about hospitality work

Do I need hospitality experience?

Not always. Support roles such as runner or dishwasher need no experience. For waiting and bar work some basic experience is a plus, but we mainly look at your attitude and motivation.

Can I choose my own shifts?

Yes. In the EXTRA app you see which shifts are available and choose what suits you. You decide when you work.

How does daily pay via Jixbee work?

After your shift you log your hours in the EXTRA app. Once they're approved, your pay is ready the next day via Jixbee. No waiting until the end of the month.

Where can I work?

With EXTRA you work at hotels, restaurants and events in Amsterdam, Utrecht and The Hague. Think Hilton, Marriott, Grand Hotel Amrâth and a range of event venues.

How soon can I start working?

After signing up and a short introduction you can soon pick up your first shifts. In most cases you're working within a week.
