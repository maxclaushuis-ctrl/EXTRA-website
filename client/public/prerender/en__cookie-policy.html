1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device to remember information, for example to keep you logged in or to measure how the website is used. In addition to cookies, some of the services listed below use similar techniques such as local storage; for convenience, we also refer to these as "cookies" below.

2. Which cookies we use

doehetextra.nl uses the following categories of cookies:

  • Necessary cookies: the session cookie extra.sid, which is needed to keep logged-in users (for example EXTRA employees or Clients) recognised during their visit. This cookie is valid for 30 days and is only placed if you log in.
  • Analytical cookies: Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) measures how many visitors the website has and how they use the website, so that we can improve the website. These cookies collect anonymised usage statistics.
  • Analytical techniques without cookies: Ahrefs Web Analytics measures visitor numbers without placing cookies or tracking individual visitors.

3. Why we use these cookies

  • To make the website work properly for logged-in users.
  • To understand which pages are well visited and where visitors get stuck, so that we can improve the website.
  • We do not use cookies for advertising purposes and do not sell data from cookies to third parties.

4. Managing and disabling cookies

You can always manage or delete cookies yourself via your browser settings. Please note that without the necessary session cookie, the website will not fully work for features that require you to log in.

You can block Google Analytics analytical cookies separately with a browser extension such as the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Changes

We may amend this cookie policy from time to time, for example when we add a new service that uses cookies. The most recent version is always available on this page, with the date of the last change shown at the top.

6. Contact

Questions about this cookie policy? Contact us at info@doehetextra.nl.
