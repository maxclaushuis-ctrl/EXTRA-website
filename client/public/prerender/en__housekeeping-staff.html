Hire in or outsource?

Hire in, outsource or employ yourself?

Under fifty rooms you keep it in-house and hire extra on busy days. Between fifty and a hundred and fifty rooms, a fixed core with a flexible layer works best. Above that, outsourcing fully can bring peace of mind, provided your occupancy is stable.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about housekeeping

Can you supply housekeeping staff?

Yes. Housekeeping is one of our core specialisms for hotels. We supply experienced housekeepers and room attendants who are familiar with hotel standards and quality checks. Through our favourites pool we build a regular team that knows your hotel inside out.

Is hiring in housekeeping more expensive than employing staff yourself?

Per hour, yes. Per year it depends on your occupancy rate. With strongly fluctuating occupancy, a fixed core plus a flexible layer is almost always cheaper than a fully permanent team you keep paying through the off-peak months.

Can I manage hired-in housekeeping staff myself?

Yes. When you hire in, the staff work precisely under your management and supervision. That is the essential difference from outsourcing. With contracted work, the supplier manages them.

Can I employ someone who works for me through a staffing agency?

Usually, yes. Check the terms and conditions for the notice period and transfer fee that apply, and agree on these at the start.

How quickly can I have housekeeping staff arranged?

For a single shift, often within a few days. If you want a fixed pool that knows your standards, allow a lead time of a few weeks.
