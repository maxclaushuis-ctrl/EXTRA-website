FAQ

Common questions about housekeeping work

Do I need experience for housekeeping work?

Experience is a plus, but not always required. We mainly look at your motivation, accuracy and attitude. For some roles we offer a short introduction on site.

Will I always work at the same hotel?

In most cases, yes. We try to place you in a regular pool at one or more hotels, so you know how things work and quickly feel at home in the team.

Can I choose my own shifts?

Yes. You tell us when you're available and we find shifts that fit. That way you can combine the work with other commitments or activities.

How does payment work?

After your shift you log your hours in the EXTRA app. Once they're approved, your pay is ready the next day via Jixbee. No waiting until the end of the month.

How soon can I start working?

After signing up and a short introduction, you can often pick up your first shifts within a week. We make sure everything runs quickly and smoothly.
