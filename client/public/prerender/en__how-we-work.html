How we work together

From introduction to a steady pool that keeps getting better on its own.

  1. 01
    We visit you in personTogether we map out your needs, goals and peak moments. We look at what you need in flexible and permanent support, at your location and at your pace.
  2. 02
    Framework agreement and onboardingBefore we start, you receive a framework agreement that meets all laws and regulations, including ABU, Wet DBA, StiPP, NEN and identity checks. Only then do we go live.
  3. 03
    Tailored sourcing and selectionWe combine three sources: our existing pool, the best-fitting candidates through targeting, and tailored sourcing when needed. Always the right match, not the fastest.
  4. 04
    Interviews and assessmentEvery candidate first comes to see us at our office. We assess attitude, presentation, experience, skills and reliability. Each candidate gets scores that feed into planning and reports.
  5. 05
    Building a steady poolWe put together a steady pool of candidates who are a good fit, are keen to work for you and match your culture and expectations.
  6. 06
    Planning and communicationYou create shifts in the portal or directly through your account manager. We take care of all the planning: always the highest scoring candidates, always on time.
  7. 07
    Review after every shiftAfter every shift, each employee is rated. The best people naturally stay in the pool. That way quality grows automatically along with your location.
  8. 08
    Continuous improvement through EXTRAATjeEmployees are rewarded through our EXTRAATje points system for small wins such as arriving on time, high ratings, the right attitude and going the extra mile.
