Agency staff, payroll or your own employees?

For a two-week peak you choose something different than for a role you want filled all year round. The difference is not in the hourly rate, but in who recruits, who the employer is and who carries the risk.

Read the difference between agency staff and freelancers
What EXTRA does

Agency staffing

We recruit and we are the employer. You manage them on the floor and pay per hour worked.

Payroll

You recruit yourself; a payroll company takes over the employer role. EXTRA does not offer this.

Your own employees

You recruit yourself and you are the employer. The logical choice for a permanent role.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are the forklift licences checked?

Always. We check that the licence is valid and record it in the file. Without a valid licence, nobody goes on a truck.

Do you also cover shift work and weekends?

Yes. Early, late and night shifts are possible, as is weekend cover during peak periods. Shift allowances follow the pay rules of your collective labour agreement, as the equal pay rules require.

Am I at risk of false self-employment?

No. Everyone who works through EXTRA is on our payroll and we are NEN 4400-1 certified. No freelance arrangements are involved.

What happens if someone does not turn up?

You call us and we find a replacement. It also counts in that employee's rating: anyone who does not turn up does not come back into your pool.

Can we try a single shift first?

You can. There is no minimum order and no notice period. You pay per hour worked.
