FAQ

Frequently asked questions about hotel staff

How quickly can you supply hotel staff?

In many cases EXTRA can supply suitable hotel staff within 48 hours. Thanks to our fixed pool of selected hotel employees we can move fast when someone drops out, at peak times or for last-minute requests. Get in touch and we'll see straight away what's possible.

Can you supply housekeeping staff?

Yes. Housekeeping is one of our core specialisms for hotels. We supply experienced housekeepers and room attendants who are familiar with hotel standards and quality checks. Through our favourites pool we build a regular team that knows your hotel inside out.

Can you supply staff for banqueting and conferences?

Yes. EXTRA supplies professional banqueting and kitchen staff for banqueting, conferences and events in hotels. From small boardroom lunches to gala dinners for 300+ guests. Our experienced staff know how formal hotel service works.

How do fixed teams per hotel work?

EXTRA works with favourites pools per client. For each hotel we build a fixed pool of employees who know your property. The same faces, less explaining, higher quality. This applies per department: housekeeping, F&B, banqueting and front office.

Which hotel roles can I hire staff for?

Through EXTRA you can hire staff for: housekeeping, front office, F&B, banqueting, kitchen (chefs, sous-chefs, kitchen staff), dishwashers and room service. We always look at which employees best fit your hotel and your standard.

How is the quality of hotel staff monitored?

After every shift we measure how an employee performed at your hotel. Scores, compliments and complaints are recorded. So we know who consistently performs well at your property and who gets priority for future requests.
