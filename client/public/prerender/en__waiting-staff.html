Which waiting staff can you hire?

From independent waiting staff with wine knowledge to runners who can handle the pace of a plated dinner for 300 guests: we match to the level your business needs. Bartenders, hosts and hostesses and banqueting staff for hotels come through the same request. Tell us in your request what service level you expect and any specific requirements (dress code, languages, fine dining experience), and we select on that.

For recurring shifts we build a regular pool: the same faces who know your menu, your floor and your house style. So to your guests, flexible staff feel like permanent staff.

Waiting staff for events and catering

Does your headcount peak around events, conferences or parties? For large productions we supply complete service teams, including an experienced point of contact on the floor. See also our pages on hiring event staff and events personnel for the full event line-up, from bar to cloakroom.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost to hire waiting staff?

You pay an all-in hourly rate that depends on experience and role. It covers wages, contributions and insurance, no hidden costs. Request a quote for your situation.

Can I ask for the same waiting staff again?

Yes. Employees you rate highly get priority when we schedule for you. That way you build a regular flex pool that knows your business.

Do you have waiting staff with wine or cocktail knowledge?

Yes, mention it in your request. We match on service level: from runners to independent waiting staff with wine knowledge and experienced bartenders.

Do you also supply for a single evening?

Absolutely, one shift is enough. Many clients start with a single evening and then scale up to a regular arrangement.
