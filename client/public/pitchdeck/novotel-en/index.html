Selection

From ad to contract, in six steps

Everyone working your floor has passed all six steps. Each step filters, and each step is on record.

Step 1

Recruitment campaign

Social campaigns around the hotel, with WhatsApp as the only button.

Step 2

WhatsApp screening

Job checks travel distance, availability, hospitality experience and language.

Step 3

In-person interview

At our office, scored on hard and soft skills. Anyone who turns up late without letting us know is out. On request, we also arrange an introduction at the hotel.

Step 4

Compliance check

ID check, right to work and confirmation that there is no other employer.

Step 5

Reference check

As standard, we call a previous employer about attendance and work ethic.

Step 6

Contract

On our payroll, with a cancellation and penalty clause in line with the collective labour agreement.

Only after step 6 can someone be scheduled. Not a moment before.
