Recruitment and selection

From ad to contract, in six steps

Everyone who works on your floor has been through all six steps. Every step filters, and every step is recorded.

Step 1

Recruitment campaign

Social ads around your site, with WhatsApp as the only button.

Step 2

WhatsApp screening

Job checks travel time, availability, certificates and right to work.

Step 3

Interview at our office

In person, scored on hard and soft skills in our dashboard.

Step 4

Compliance check

ID check, right to work and confirmation of no other employer.

Step 5

Reference check

We call a previous employer about attendance and attitude.

Step 6

Contract

On our payroll, with a cancellation and penalty clause in line with the CAO.

Only after step 6 can someone be scheduled. Not before.
